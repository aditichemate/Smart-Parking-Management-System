Step 1: Start the program.

Step 2: Initialize the linked list with Head = NULL.

Step 3: Display the main menu.

Step 4: Accept the user's choice.

Step 5: If the choice is 1, enter vehicle details and add the vehicle to the linked list.

Step 6: If the choice is 2, enter the vehicle number and remove the vehicle from the linked list.

Step 7: If the choice is 3, enter the vehicle number and search for the vehicle.

Step 8: If the choice is 4, display all parked vehicles.

Step 9: If the choice is 5, exit the program.

Step 10: Repeat Steps 3–9 until the user selects Exit.

Step 11: Stop the program.
